# Coding Schedule
---
## Daily Routine of an Expert DEV
---
1. **Plan what are you going to do for the day**, before write any single line of code, plan what's the goal to reach at the end of the day.
2. **50/10 Rule**, means that you are going to work for a time of 50 minutes and then have a break from the screen for 10 minutes.
>[!warning]
>**Do not look at another screen.** Your brain needs this quiet time to process the logic you just wrote.
3. **Review the code you wrote**, check if any part of the code works and satisfy the goal you plan for the session / end of the day.
